All about Me

My name is Arleah Carole Bien M. Oga. According to my family I have 3 names to compensate for my 3 letter family name. Arleah comes from my maternall grandparents names. 'Ar-' for Arthur and '-leah' for Aquelia. Carole was made by my father, it was supposed to be just 'Carol' which means a song, but they want it to be slightly unique that is why they added an 'e' at the end. My third name 'Bien' means 'good' in french, which technically makes my name 'a good song'.

I am the 1st child, the 1st daughter, the 1st granddaughter on both sides of my family, and the 1st great-granddaughter on my maternal family. This may seem like it comes with a lot of pressure but it does not. My family has not pressured my greatly, they only want me to study hard and be able to achieve what I want to become. The only pressure that I have felt from them was when they urged me to pursue nursing even if I did not want to. But now I have adjusted to the profession and has started to like it a little.
